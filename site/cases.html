Cases

Judgment is most useful when the evidence is incomplete and the answer is not yet supportable.

Each case makes the question, evidence boundary, relevant professional lenses, and unresolved issues visible. We do not turn an internal validation record into a marketing promise.

THE QUESTIONWhat changed in the decision?Start with the real decision, not the requested deliverable.
THE EVIDENCEWhat is actually available?Separate material signals from convenient assumptions.
THE CHALLENGEWhat should be tested?Bring in the right lens; do not add specialists by reflex.
THE RESULTWhat remains unresolved?Unresolved is a legitimate result when the evidence says so.
We preserve the part of the work most businesses are tempted to hide: the edge of what can honestly be said.
