A bounded first step

Test the judgment.

Bring one real business question. You do not need a perfect brief.

Start with the question that got harder.

Bring a live decision, a difficult trade-off, or a question that changed because the situation changed. We clarify the decision, identify the work that is actually needed, and show where professional judgment can help.

1. Bring the context

What decision is pending? What changed? What would make the next step safer?

2. Bound the work

Separate what needs judgment from what only needs information, process, or a clear stop.

3. Return the decision

Make the evidence, challenge, uncertainty, and human authorization visible.

No invented trial inbox. No promise that every problem should become a model.
