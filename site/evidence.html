Method and boundary

Evidence before certainty.

Clear boundaries build trust.

Our public material distinguishes between what is demonstrated, what is being validated, and what is not claimed.

What we preserve

  • Incomplete evidence and material contradictions
  • Assumptions that require challenge
  • Unresolved judgment and explicit uncertainty
  • The point at which a human decision is required

What we do not claim

We do not claim autonomous decision-making, production readiness, or general effectiveness beyond the cases and boundaries that are actually supported.

Public evidence uses stable canonical references only. Internal working branches, private runtime records, and unpublished drafts are not public proof.
